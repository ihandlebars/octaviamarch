Octavia March

Audio Engineer | Radio Imager | Media Producer

Octavia March is a powerhouse in audio engineering, video editing, podcast production, and content creation — with over a decade of proven success shaping sound for major radio networks, television platforms, and global brands.

A graduate of The American InterContinental University with a BFA in Media Production, Octavia has mastered the intricacies of audio software and hardware engineering. In 2009, she founded iHandlebars Production, a boutique audio production company providing top-tier imaging, engineering, and sound design services to radio stations across the U.S., Africa, and the Bahamas.

Her career took off at Atlanta's top-rated V103 radio station, where she rose from imaging producer to director, lending her creative expertise to shows like The Ryan Cameron Show, Cannon's Countdown with Nick Cannon, and Live In The Den with Big Tigger. She also co-created and produced The Loud Pack with DJ Kash and Lil Bankhead, showcasing her versatility across formats.

Octavia later became studio manager and lead engineer at Sony Music / Bad Boy South's Block Entertainment, where she further cemented her status in the industry. Her credits include producing the NBA's Atlanta Hawks Code of Conduct Song, working as assistant producer at Stankonia Studios, and contributing to projects for The Laugh Out Loud Network, where she produced original music scores for digital content.

She most recently served as show producer, cinematographer and podcast editor for The Baller Alert Show on Revolt TV, where she directed visuals, produced content, and edited episodes for digital platforms — blending her technical precision with creative vision. In addition, she's held the role of imaging director for stations including Mix 87.7 Old School & R&B and Atlanta's Streetz 94.5, consistently shaping the sonic identity of top-tier radio.

Known for her professionalism, precision, and industry-savvy leadership, Octavia March continues to break barriers in a male-dominated field. She brings unmatched creative vision, technical skill, and executive-level experience to every project — making her an invaluable asset to any production team.
